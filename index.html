Car rental booking system

Car rental business that takes bookings through Facebook

Bookings lived in a Google Sheet, and the same car sometimes got booked twice. Customers now pick a car, see free dates and send a request from the link on the Facebook page.

  • The database itself blocks double bookings
  • Customers upload GCash or bank payment screenshots; the owner gets a phone notification
  • Printable rental contracts, car check-out and check-in photos
  • Renter IDs kept in private storage, in line with the Data Privacy Act
  • Next.js
  • Postgres (Neon)
  • Drizzle
  • Web push

How a project goes

Most systems go live in one to three weeks.

  1. We talk

    You show me how the business runs today: the notebook, the spreadsheet, the problems.

  2. I write the plan

    A short list of screens and features, in plain words, with a price. You approve it before I build.

  3. I build in steps

    You test each part on your own phone or tablet and tell me what to change.

  4. You own it

    Everything moves to accounts in your name. I train your staff and stay on for support.

You deal with me directly from the first talk to the handover. I plan it, build it, test it on your devices and train your staff myself.
